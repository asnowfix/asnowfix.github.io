5. Your data and backups

homedrive can create, overwrite and delete files, both locally and in Google Drive, as part of keeping the two in sync. When both sides change, the default newer_wins policy keeps the older version with a .old.<N> suffix. Deletions sent to Google Drive go to the Drive trash by default. homedrive is not a backup service. Keep your own backups of anything you cannot afford to lose.
