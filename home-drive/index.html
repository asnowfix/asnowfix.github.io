homedrive

Self-hosted, two-way Google Drive sync for headless Linux

homedrive is a free, open-source sync agent. It keeps a folder on your own Linux machine, such as a Raspberry Pi NAS, in sync with your own Google Drive, in both directions.

What it does

  • Watches your local folder and uploads changes to Google Drive.
  • Polls the Google Drive Changes API and downloads changes made elsewhere.
  • Treats the local disk as the source of truth and works offline.
  • Resolves conflicts with "newer wins", keeping the older version as .old.<N>.
  • Optionally publishes status to your own MQTT broker for Home Assistant.

How it uses your Google data

homedrive runs entirely on hardware you control. It uses the Google Drive API only to sync your files between your machine and your own Drive. There is no homedrive server, no telemetry and no analytics, and nothing is sent to the maintainer. Your OAuth token is stored only on your machine. See the privacy policy for details.
